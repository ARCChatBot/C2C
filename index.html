C2C Training Management System Architecture

Layer 01

Computer-Based Training Node

Hands-on simulations and an electronics matching game covering cleanroom, safety, fabrication, and responsible AI use.

Layer 02

3D Simulation Training Node

Immersive 3D environments simulating semiconductor fabrication processes.

Layer 03

VR Cleanroom Training Node

Virtual Reality integration guiding students through cleanroom protocols.

Layer 04

AR Data Visualization Node

Web-based augmented reality for training reports and immersive data experiences. Explore results as 3D charts placed in your space, right from the browser.

  • Interactive 3D report dashboards
  • Place data in your room on a phone, tablet, or headset
  • No app install — runs in a web browser
Layer 05

SOP & Documentation Requests

Standard Operating Procedures and safety documents are shared by email. Request the ones you need through Outlook.

  1. 1Choose the documents you need.
  2. 2Outlook opens with your request already written.
  3. 3Send it from your VSU email account.
Layer 06

3D Printer & Equipment Training & Scheduling

1Training Times: Tuesdays & Thursdays from 3:30 PM – 4:50 PM EST.
2Equipment: Digital Multimeter, HoloLens 2, VR Oculus 2, Enderbot 3D Printer, NVIDIA Jetson Nano.
3Trainer Sign-Off: Assigned student mentor must sign off on the session during hands-on training.
