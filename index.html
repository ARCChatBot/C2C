C2C Training Management System Architecture

Layer 01

Computer-Based Training Node

Interactive digital modules delivering foundational knowledge and AI literacy.

Launch CBT Portal
Layer 02

3D Simulation Training Node

Immersive 3D environments simulating semiconductor fabrication processes.

Layer 03

VR Cleanroom Training Node

Virtual Reality integration guiding students through cleanroom protocols.

Layer 04

SOP & Documentation Repository

Centralized repository for Standard Operating Procedures and safety guidelines with viewable PDF viewer.
